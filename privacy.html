Privacy Notice

Barangay Rosario (“the Barangay”) is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

What we collect

Name, sex, date and place of birth, civil status, address, household information, contact details, photo, occupation, sector membership (e.g. senior citizen, PWD, solo parent, 4Ps), government-issued IDs and other documents you upload, payment records, and records of incidents and complaints you are involved in.

Why we collect it

  • To maintain the Registry of Barangay Inhabitants (RBI) and household records;
  • To process your applications for clearances, certificates, IDs and permits;
  • To record incident reports and settle disputes under the Katarungang Pambarangay;
  • To deliver social services, plan programs and respond to disasters; and
  • To send you status updates by email and in the resident portal.

Who can access it

Only authorized barangay officials and staff, according to their role. Every access and change is recorded in an audit trail. Uploaded documents are stored privately and never published. The public document-verification page shows only masked names and document details.

How long we keep it

Records are kept for as long as you are a resident and as required by law and government records-retention rules, after which they are securely disposed of.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to erasure or blocking (when allowed by law), to data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
