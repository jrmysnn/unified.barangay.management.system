Services & Citizen's Charter

Requirements, fees, steps and processing time for every barangay service.

Certifies that the resident has no pending case or derogatory record in the barangay. Used for employment, loans, travel and other requirements.

Fee
₱100.00
Processing time
1 working day(s)
Valid for
180 days

What to prepare

  • Valid government-issued ID
    Front side, clear photo or scan

Steps

  1. Sign in to your resident account (or register first).
  2. Choose the service and fill in the short form.
  3. Upload clear photos or scans of the requirements.
  4. Wait for the barangay to review – you will be notified.
  5. Pay the fee (if any) at the Treasurer's window or online.
  6. Claim your document at the barangay hall, or print it if allowed.

Apply for Special Permits

Before you apply

  • You need a verified resident account. Register here – it takes about 5 minutes.
  • Use clear, readable photos of your documents (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB).
  • Processing time is counted in working days, from the day your requirements are complete.
  • Bring your reference number when claiming your document.
