Barangay Clearance

Certifies that the resident has no pending case or derogatory record in the barangay. Used for employment, loans, travel and other requirements.

₱100.00 1 working day

Certificate of Indigency

Certifies that the resident belongs to an indigent family, for medical, burial, educational or legal assistance.

Free 1 working day

How it works

Four simple steps from your phone or computer.

Register

Create your free account and upload a valid ID or proof of residency.

Get verified

The Barangay Secretary checks your details – you will be notified.

Apply & pay

Choose a service, upload requirements, and pay at the hall or online.

Claim

Get notified when ready. Every document has a QR code anyone can verify.

Is this barangay document genuine?

Every clearance, certificate, ID and permit we issue carries a QR code and control number. Employers, banks and schools can check it here in seconds.

  • Scan the QR code with any phone camera, or
  • Type the control number printed on the document.
